The Department of Soil Science started with the establishment of Hisar Campus of the erstwhile Punjab Agricultural University. Dr. D.R. Bhumla took over as first Head of the department on 26th August 1963. The Department has attained the distinction of one of the biggest departments of the College of Agriculture with faculty strength of approximately 135 including 112 teachers/scientists/extension specialists of Soil Science discipline during 1982-83 when Dr. Manoj Kumar Sharma was the Head of the Department and presently with strength of about 23 faculty members. 

Mandate/Objectives:

  • To impart teaching in the field of Soil Science to under graduate and post graduate students
  • To conduct research in different disciplines of Soil Science including Pedology, Soil Survey, Remote Sensing, Soil Fertility, Soil Chemistry, Soil Physics, Water Management, Soil Salinity, Soil Pollution and in any new area depending on requirement of the state.
  • Transfer of technology to the farmers through KVKs and government agencies.

Changes in its nomenclature: Initially, the name of the department was ‘Soils’ which changed to ‘Soil Science’ in 1986.
