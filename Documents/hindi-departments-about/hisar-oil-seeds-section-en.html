The Oilseeds Section at CCS HAU Hisar, is a prominent department dedicated to the research and development of various oilseed crops varieties. It plays a crucial role in advancing agricultural practices related to oilseed production, addressing challenges, and exploring innovative solutions.

Under the guidance of esteemed scientists and experts, the Oilseeds Section conducts extensive research to enhance the productivity, quality, and sustainability of oilseed crops. This includes the development of different oilseed varieties, their genetic traits, and their adaptation to varying climatic conditions. The section also focuses on developing improved cultivation techniques, disease and pest management strategies, and efficient post-harvest handling methods.

The researchers at the Oilseeds Section actively collaborate with farmers, agricultural agencies, and industry stakeholders to understand the real-world challenges faced by oilseed growers. By conducting on-farm trials and demonstrations, they aim to transfer their scientific knowledge and technologies directly to the farmers, promoting the adoption of best practices and increasing overall agricultural productivity.

Furthermore, the Oilseeds Section serves as a platform for knowledge dissemination through seminars, workshops, and training programs. It actively engages with students, researchers, and aspiring agriculturists, encouraging them to explore the vast potential of oilseed crops and contribute to the field of agricultural sciences.

With its strong commitment to research excellence, the Oilseeds Section at CCS HAU Hisar plays a vital role in addressing the ever-growing demand for edible oils and diversifying the agricultural landscape. Its contributions have a far-reaching impact on the agricultural community, ensuring sustainable oilseed production and food security in the region and beyond.
