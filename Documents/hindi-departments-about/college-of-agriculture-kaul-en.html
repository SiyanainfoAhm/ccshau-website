The college of Agriculture, Kaul is an integral part and accredited to CCS HAU, Hisar. It was taken over from Kurukshetra University and started in 1972 with the idea to impart quality and scientific training to students in the field of agriculture. The first admission to this college was made in 1974. Initially this college imparted education for the first years i.e. 1 and 2year of 5 year programme and after that, the students were migrated to the main campus at CCSHAU, Hisar for completion of their degree programme. It attained the status of a full fledged college after up-gradation in 1983. The first batch of B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture degree passed out in 1986. Till 2016 a total of 1224 students including 47 of the current year have graduated from this college. At present the college has 176 students on roll including 53 girls with an annual intake of 50 students. The college has a competent faculty of 10 teachers to impart quality education and 52 supporting non-teaching staff.
