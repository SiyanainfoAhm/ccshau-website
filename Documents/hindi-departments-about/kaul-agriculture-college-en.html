The college of Agriculture, Kaul is an integral part and accredited to CCS HAU, Hisar. It was taken over from Kurukshetra University and started in 1972 with the idea to impart quality and scientific training to students in the field of agriculture. The first admission to this college was made in 1974. Initially this college imparted education for the first years i.e. 1 and 2 year of 5 year programme and after that, the students were migrated to the main campus at CCSHAU, Hisar for completion of their degree programme. It attained the status of a fully fledged college after up-gradation in 1983. The first batch of B.Sc. (Hons) Agriculture degree passed out in 1986. The college has thus far contributed around 1513 well-trained graduates to the human resource pool of agriculture and allied sectors.  Many alumni of the college have risen to exalted positions and gained recognition both at national and international levels. At present, there are 211 students on roll including 107 girls admitted for B.Sc.(Hons) Agri. 4- year programe and Post graduate programme.

College of Agriculture, Kaul situated on the outskirts of village Kaul, district Kaithal, Haryana (29.8498o N latitude, 76.6615o E longitude) is an importantconstituent college of Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University (CCS HAU), Hisar. College of Agriculture, Kaul has its beginning as a constituent collegeof Kurukshetra University, Kurukshetra in the erstwhilestate of joint Punjab. Aftercreation of the state of Haryana in 1966, HaryanaAgricultural University came into being at Hisar in the year 1970. The College of Agriculture, Kaul was integrated into the Haryana Agricultural University in 1974 with the motto to impart quality and scientific educationto students in the field of agriculture.

Initially, the academic session was started for B.Sc. (Hons) Agri. having both i.e. 4 year and 5 year programmes at Kaul campus. The classes for 1st year under 4- year programme, and 1st and 2nd year under 5-year programme were initiated at Kaul campus and the rest of classes were conducted at the main campus of HAU, Hisar. Dr. M.S.Chaudhary was the founder Principal who served the college from 28-7-1974 to 11-5-1977 and thereafter eighteeneminent Professors headedthe post of Principal till date, under whose leadership the college has made significant achievements in the field of academics, sports, NCC/NSS and other co-curricular activities. College of Agriculture, Kaul was upgraded to a full-fledged college in 1983 and the first batch completed their graduation in 1986. It is a matter of pride that during the period under consideration (April 2018 to March 2023) from a total of 15 University Gold Medals awarded to the studentsof B.Sc. (Hons.)Agriculture stream in the University, 07 medals have been won by the students of College of Agriculture, Kaul. Many students won laurels in national level competitions in sports and other co-curricular activities like NCC/NSS etc.

During this long journey of 49 years, a total of 1513 students have completed their B.Sc. (Hons) Agri. degree from this esteemed college. Presently, 211 students including 107 girls are enrolled for B.Sc. (Hons) Agri. 4- year programe. Later, postgraduate programme of M.Sc. (Agriculture) and Ph.D. was initiated in threedisciplines of Agronomy, Soil Science and Genetics & Plant Breedingin the year 2019-20. Presently, 12 M.Sc. studentsand 9 Ph.D. students are enrolled in this college.
