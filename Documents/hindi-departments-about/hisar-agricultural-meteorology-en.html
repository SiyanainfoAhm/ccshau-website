The Department of Agricultural Meteorology was established on 16 February, 1982 as per the recommendations of National Commission on Agriculture. Since then, an excellent infrastructure has been created for teaching/research/extension in Agricultural Meteorology viz., Agricultural climatology, Crop-weather-pest-disease interactions, Crop growth & yield simulation modeling, Climatic Change & Variability, Remote Sensing & GIS Applications and Weather forecasting based Agro-advisories. Agricultural Meteorology is an applied science, which quantify the interaction between physical environment (Weather) on one hand and agriculture (Field crops, Vegetable crops Horticultural crops, Dairy Farming, Poultryetc.) on other hand.
