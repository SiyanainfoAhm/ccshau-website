All India Coordinated Pearl Millet Improvement Project started functioning since 1969 as main centre.  The major objective of the project has been to evaluate pearl millet varieties and hybrids combining high yield with high degree of resistance to drought and diseases with suitable maturity and to develop suitable production and protection technologies. With the funds available from State Government and limited funds from Promotion and Development Efforts on Hybrids in Selected Crops – Pearl Millet work is also carried out and strengthened on other aspects like development of hybrids, male sterile lines, restorers, populations, top cross hybrids etc., development of seed production technology and popularization of production technology.

 An interdisciplinary team of four scientists and two STAs is engaged in improvement work on pearl millet. Presently eight schemes are operative in Bajra Section and bajra improvement work at CCS HAU, Hisar.
