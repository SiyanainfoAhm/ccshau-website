The Department of Aquaculture was established at Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University in 2019 concurrent with the foundation of the College of Fisheries Science. The department has been dedicatedly advancing sustainable and responsible practices within the fisheries sector. Numerous initiatives have been successfully implemented to bolster the sustainability of aquaculture operations. Through rigorous research and innovative approaches, culture practices for various fish species, shrimps, freshwater mussels, and optimized feed formulations have been developed to enhance feed efficiency and minimize environmental impacts, including fish culture in sewage-treated water.Additionally, technologies for aquaponics, Azolla, and Spirulina culture have been standardized. Looking forward, investments are being made in research and development to further refine the efficiency, environmental performance, and welfare standards of aquaculture systems. Explorations are underway into innovative technologies such as recirculating aquaculture systems, bio floc systems, and integrated carp-ornamental culture aquaculture to bolster the resilience and productivity of farmed species. Since the 2019-20 academic year, the Department has been offering M.F.Sc. and Ph.D. degree programs in Aquaculture.
