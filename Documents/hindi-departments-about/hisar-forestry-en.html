Department of Forestry was established on 16.2.1982 to strengthen farm forestry research. Later,B.Sc. Forestry 4 year programme was started from 1986-87 as an initiative from ICAR with the objective to produce forestry trained graduates. However, B.Sc. (Forestry) was discontinued from academic year 1990-91 due to insufficient demands of forestry trained personnel in the forestry sector. However the situation has changed now due to flourishing of wood industries which require particular forestry trained graduates to fulfill their multidimensional demands that arise from various forestry sectors.Up to 1990- 1993, total 55 students completed B.Sc. forestry degree programme in four batches. 

M.Sc. Agroforestry programme was started from 1990-91, with intake capacity of 3 students and from 1998-99 along with M.Sc. Agroforestry, Ph.D. Agroforestry Programme was also started. 87 students have completed M.Sc. and whereas30 students have got Ph.D. degree from the Department of Forestry. Presently, Department of Forestryhas M.Sc. Forestryand Ph.D. Forestryprogrammes under College of Agriculture. The department offers two undergraduate core courses under commercial Agriculture. The department of Forestry will also offer two undergraduate core courses as per 5th Dean’s committee recommendations. At PG level the department offers eleven M.Sc and eight Ph.D. courses.

The department has well qualifiedand experienced facultymembers having vast experience and international exposure. All faculty members have obtained PhD degree. Faculty members are using latest literature and books for teaching to UG and PG classes. Teachers are using modern audio-visual aids & proper equipment for practical demonstration. Major emphasis is given for practical learning. Faculty of the Department is actively involved in academic pursuits at National and International levels.
