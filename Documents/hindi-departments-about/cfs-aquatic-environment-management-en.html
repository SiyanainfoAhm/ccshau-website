The Department of Aquatic Environment Management is at the forefront of ensuring the health and sustainability of aquatic ecosystems. Offering M.F.Sc. and Ph.D. degree programs, our department plays a crucial role in preserving the delicate balance of aquatic environments, safeguarding biodiversity, and mitigating environmental risks.Our dedicated team is actively engaged in monitoring and assessing aquatic ecosystems, identifying potential threats, and implementing effective management strategies to maintain water quality and ecological integrity. Continuous research and professional development enable us to stay ahead of emerging environmental challenges, ensuring that we can provide the best possible stewardship for aquatic habitats.As a key player in environmental conservation, the Department collaborates with various stakeholders to address issues such as pollution, habitat degradation, and invasive species management. Through our efforts, we strive to promote sustainable practices and enhance the resilience of aquatic ecosystems for the benefit of both present and future generations.
