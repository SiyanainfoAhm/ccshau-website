The Department of Fish Engineering, established in 2020 within the College of Fisheries Science at CCS Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar, offers comprehensive Bachelor's and Master's programs encompassing fishing gear technology, fishing craft technology, aquaculture engineering, refrigeration and equipment engineering, fishing technology, navigation, and seamanship. Engaged in teaching, research, and extension activities, the department focuses on various aspects including pond design and construction, fishing gear materials and design, fabrication of fishing gear, responsible fishing methods, and marine engineering. Additionally, it covers topics such as navigation equipment, sea safety, refrigeration engineering, fish processing machinery, and aquaculture engineering. Our primary objective is to nurture skilled technical and professional personnel for the field of Fish Engineering. Recognized as a cornerstone department in the fishing industry, we are committed to advancing education, research, and practices that contribute to the sustainable development of fisheries and aquaculture sectors.
