KVK Sadalpur is located 42 Km away from Hisar in the North-Western direction and is connected to MandiAdampur by metalled road (9 Km from MandiAdampur). MandiAdampur is connected both by train and road from Hisar. Hisar district is a part of the Indo-Gangetic alluvial plain, covering an area of 4467 sqkms, lies between 250 59 to 26049 N latitude and 750 11 to 76018E longitude and is bordered by Fatehabad district of Haryana in the North, Bhadra (Distt. Hanumangarh) and Rajgarh (Distt. Churu) of Rajasthan in the South & West direction, Tosham (Bhiwani Distt.) in the South and Jind&Rohtak districts in the Eastern direction. Hisar district is comprised of two sub-divisions viz., Hisar and Hansi, four sub tehsils viz., Narnaund, UklanaMandi, MandiAdampur and Barwala and nine developmental blocks namely: Adampur, Barwala, Hansi, Baas, Hisar-I, Hisar-II, Narnaund, Agroha and Uklana. The principal agriculture markets are: Hisar, Hansi, Adampur, Uklana and Barwala.
