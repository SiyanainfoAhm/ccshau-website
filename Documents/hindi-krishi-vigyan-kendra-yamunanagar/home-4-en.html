Krishi Vigyan Kendra, Damla Yamuna Nagar was sanctioned by Indian Council of Agriculture Research and was established on August 1st 1992 under the administrative control of Directorate of Extension Education, CCS, Haryana Agriculture University, Hisar.

            KVK, Damla is located about 7 km. away from Yamuna Nagar on Yamunanagar-Karnal Highway. Gram Panchayat Damla donated 59 acres of old land at Village Damla to develop infrastructure facilities like demonstration units, administrative block, farmer’s hostel, lab and agriculture farm.  KVK is actively engaged in increasing agricultural productivity and sustainability based on the mandates of KVK.
