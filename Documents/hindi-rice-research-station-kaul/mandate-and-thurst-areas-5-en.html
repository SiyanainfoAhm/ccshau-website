Mandates of Rice Research Station, Kaul

  • Development of non-scented and scented rice varieties with higher yield, good grain quality and multiple resistance to major biotic stresses.
  • Development of water and nutrient efficient rice genotypes suitable under transplanted and direct seeded conditions.
  • Development of cultural practices for new rice varieties.
  • Development of integrated nutrient management and resource conservation technology to enhance input use efficiency in rice.
  • Identification of effective pesticides and agrochemicals for management of major biotic and abiotic stresses in rice.
  •  Development of integrated pest management practices in rice.

Emerging/thrust areas

  • Development of climate resilient rice cultivars for direct seeded (aerobic) and transplanted conditions with resistance to major biotic and abiotic stresses.
  • Development of improved agronomic and integrated nutrient management practices in rice for improving input use efficiency and soil health under rice-wheat cropping system.
  • Enhancing water use efficiency in rice through use of water saving technology like DSR in rice under rice-wheat cropping system.
  • Identification of effective bio-fertilizers and bio-pesticides for organic farming in rice.
  • Development of integrated pest management practices for management of major/emerging biotic stresses in rice.
  • Development of appropriate mechanism for effective management of Southern Rice Black Streak Dwarf virus.
  • Providing district wise weather based agro-advisory to farmers to plan/guide day to day field operations.
