KVK Bhiwani is situated opposite Bhim stadium. Bhiwani district lies between latitude 280 20’ to 290 05’ and longitude 750 28’ to 760 25’, respectively. The district is bounded by district Hisar in North, Mahendergarh in South, Rohtak & Jhajjar in East and by Rajasthan state in West. It is the largest district of Haryana covering an area about 4655.04 sq. km. with population about 1424554. The district comprises of five sub-divisions viz. Bhiwani, Charkhi Dadri, Loharu, Siwani and Tosham, six tehsils viz. Bhiwani, Bawani Khera, Ch. Dadri, Loharu, Siwani and Tosham and ten blocks viz. Bhiwani, Bawani Khera, Badhra, Behal, Ch. Dadri-1, Ch. Dadri-II, Loharu, Kairu, Siwani and Tosham. Total number of village in the district is 444.
