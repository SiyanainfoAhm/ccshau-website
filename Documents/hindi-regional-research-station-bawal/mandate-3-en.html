Mandate

1. To generate production and protection technology for various cropping/farming/ horticultural/ agro-forestry systems      for rainfed,limited irrigation and brackish water conditions.

2. Improvement of major cereal, pulse, oilseed, fodder and horticultural crops.

3. Development of technology for improvement of soil conservation and management of water.

4. Survey, introduction and evaluation of indigenous and exotic material of crops and arid fruits.

5. To develop post harvest technology for arid fruits.

6. Development of Agri-Silvi-Horti system for South-West Haryana

7. To produce quality seeds of crops and saplings of fruit trees.

8. Transfer of technology.

Thrust areas:

  • Development of high yielding varieties/hybrids of various crops (pearl millet, groundnut, mung bean, cluster bean, castor, wheat, barley, rapeseed- mustard, gram etc.) for emerging farming systems with low inputs, tolerant to biotic/abiotic stresses and value addition/industrial suitability.
  • Development of suitable crop production, protection, climate resilient technologies and crop management practices to reduce yield gap with effective management of natural resources in view of global climate change.
  • Crop diversification with crop like castor for increasing total production.
  • Development of natural and organic farming technologies for food crops in S-W Haryana.
  • Promoting conservation agriculture and precision farming including water saving technology like zero tillage, bed planting, seed priming, drip irrigation, fertigation and residue management. 
  • Identification of fast growing multipurpose tree species and evaluation of different agro-forestry, agri-silvi-horticulture systems suitable for S-W Haryana. 
  • Identification, evaluation, conservation and multiplication of elite germplasm/cultivars of arid zone fruits.
  • Survey, surveillance and monitoring of new emerging weeds/pests/diseases.
