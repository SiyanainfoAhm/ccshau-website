KVK, Kaithal under the able guidance and support of district administration, Kaithal, established a district level Farmer Club in the year 2000. The technical support to the activities of club is provided by this center. This club is the first of its kind in Haryana and even in the northern region.  After showing the activities and successes of this club, the same experience was repeated in each and every district of Haryana under the instructions of Director of Agriculture, Haryana.  Monthly meeting of the club is being held on fifth of every month.  The time specific and relevant topics are discussed in these meetings under the expertise of this center.  The meetings are held at the farm of any successful entrepreneurs who have achieved success in some areas so that the visiting farmers can see the socio-economic viability of the enterprise and benefit from experience of the farmers as seeing is believing.
