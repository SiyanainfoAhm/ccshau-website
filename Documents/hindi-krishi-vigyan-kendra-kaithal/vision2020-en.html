The Krishi Vigyan Kendra Kaithal intends to concentrate on one important allied agricultural vocation in one village involving all the line departments and financial institutions of the district.  Maximum number of farmers will be trained and helped to adopt that vocation. If approved by the Scientific Advisory Committee and permitted by the financing and administrative authorities concerned the VAN GRAM, MADHU GRAM, KHUMB GRAM, DUGDH GRAM, COMPOST GRAM.FAL GRAM AND SAMRIDH GRAM will be established in the operational area.
