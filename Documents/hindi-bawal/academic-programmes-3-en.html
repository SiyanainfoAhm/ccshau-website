Academic programmes:

The college is offering the following programmes:

  • B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture 6-year programme after 10th with science
  • M.Sc. (Ag.) in Agronomy, Soil Science, Plant Pathology, Entomology and Forestry. 
  • Ph.D. in  Agronomy, Soil Science, Plant Pathology, Entomology and Forestry

Admission Policy:

Admission to the college is based on the merit list of the entrance examination conducted by the university. The details of the admission and procedure are available on university website, www.hau.ac.in

Intake Capacity:

Current intake capacity of college for 4- year programme is 25 and for 6-year programme is 55. College is also admitting 12 students in M.Sc. and 6 in Ph.D. in different disciplines 

Note:- Seats may increased /decreased depending upon the need, demand and facilities available
