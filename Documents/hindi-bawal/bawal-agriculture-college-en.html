GOVERNANCE:

The college is a constituent unit of Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar.

Facilities and infrastructure             

College of Agriculture, Bawal consists of newly constructed spacious academic block having cross ventilated Smart lecture rooms, separate labs for each subject, computer lab, air conditioned Seminar Hall and library.

Hostel Facilities 

There are two well Furnished hostels one each for girls and boys. Both hostels have separate mess, common room and gym facilities for students. The mess is co-operative type managed by the student themselves. English and Hindi newspapers and competitive magazines are regularly subscribed in the common rooms. Televisions with D2H connections are also provided in each Hostel for entertainment of students. Indoor game facilities like Chess, Table tennis, Carom board etc are available in hostel. Separate hostel wardens and attendants are appointed to take care of the welfare of students. 

Co-curricular Activities 

One senior Faculty member is designated as Assistant Director of Students Welfare (ADSW) to cater to needs of all Co- curricular Activities such as sports, NCC, NSS, Mountaineering and Adventure, Music, Dance, dramatics, debate, personality development etc. for promotion of these activities. The ADSW always keeps live liaison with DSW office at main campus (Hisar) where regular classes, training/coaching, various training/workshops are arranged from time to time.

Medical Facilities

Medical facilities are provided to students and staff at the campus. The college has a qualified part doctor and well established dispensary with Para-medical staff.

Solar Lights:

As a green and environment friendly initiative, the campus has been put under 100 solar lights.
