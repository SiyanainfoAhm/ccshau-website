1. Hostel: The Institute has a well furnished 24 rooms hostel for providing lodging facilities to the participants. The hostel is used for participants of various On-campus courses organized at the Institute. It also serves as a guest house for various dignitaries visiting the institute for official purposes. All the rooms in the hostel have attached bath, geyser, A.C and LED along with dining facilities. Facility of Wi-Fi is also available in the hostel premises.
  2. Seminar Hall: The Institute has a seminar hall having capacity of 45-50 participants. It is being used for imparting training to the participants of various courses
  3. Library: The Institute has a good collection of books on Agricultural Extension and allied subjects. In addition to about 3900 books, the library is also enriched with about 15 quarterly/ monthly journals and magazines. 
  4. Computer Lab: The institute has a computer lab having 20 computers of latest technology, internet connectivity and a projector for the training purpose
