Knowledge and information are the most essential inputs of the era and must be effectively communicated to the farmers to respond successfully to the opportunities & challenges of social economic & technological changes for the improvement of agricultural productivity, food security & rural livelihoods. Agricultural Technology Information Centre (ATIC) popularly known as ‘Kisan Seva Kendra’came into existence at CCS Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar in February, 2002. ATIC is situated near Gate No. 4 of CCS HAU, Hisar which is easily approachable to the farmers for taking the inputs in form of crop seeds, vegetable seeds, university literature and advisory services. Farm advisory services are provided to the farmers and other growers through Tollfree Helpline Number (18001803001 on Monday, Wednesday & Friday from 10:00am to 1:00pm). Recently Virtual Krishi Samasaya Samadhan Seva has also been started on the day of Toll free helpline through google meet link : ftf-bkqu- mae. Farmers can interact with resource person through this link, by showing live samples of their field problems (insect-pests, disease and other problems etc.).
