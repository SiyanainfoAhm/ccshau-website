MoU between CCSHAU, Hisar and Agriculture Skill Council of India (ASCI)

The Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was made on 6th day of December, 2016 between CCSHAU, Hisar and Agriculture Skill Council of India. Saina Nehwal Institute of Agricultural Technology, Training & Education of Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University has been made a training centre and training partner of Agriculture Skill Council of India (ASCI). The Centre SNIATTE has been registered by ASCI SDMS Reg. No NSDC-REG No. 16670 and has been allotted as training centre ID 107787 to award Certificate/Diploma of skill development in 16 different disciplines. One training for Master Trainers’ has been organized from January 03-05, 2018. Twenty scientists from various disciplines from College of Agriculture and Agricultural Engineering have been certified as “Master Trainers”. The course content being followed for various job roles was further improved and supplemented under various sub-heads of the topic by the Master Trainers of CCSHAU, Hisar from various departments of COA & COAE & T on the pattern of ASCI was approved in the 479th (Emergent) meeting of The Academic Council, CCSHAU, Hisar held on 27.10.2018 and approved vide No. Acad./18/A-1/ACP/35927-49 dated 6.11.18. 

MoU between CCSHAU, Hisar and Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes (CIRB)

MoU was made on 10.10.2019 between Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar as first party and ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes, Hisar as an second party for organization of skill trainings under SCSP prpgramme for the farmers/members of SC community. Wherein, the CCSHAU, Hisar will conduct these trainings ICAR-CIRB would meet out all expenses going to be incurred in organizing these trainings. The SINATTE, Director of Extension Education, CCSHAU, Hisar shall provide all the facilities including (faculty and infrastructure) for conductance of theses trainings.
