Farmers FIRST Scheme 

The Farmers FIRST (Farmer’s Farm, Innovations, Resources, Science and Technology  programme as a concept of ICAR is developed as farmer in a centric role for research problem identification, prioritization and conduct of experiments and its management at farmers’ conditions. The focus is on Farmer’s Farm, Innovations, Resources, Science and Technology (FIRST). Integrated agriculture development is based more on efficient resource management. Keeping this in view, 124 the present project is being implemented to give desired focus to the farmers’ needs, resources and local environment. The farmers will be treated as an active partner in the development and transfer of technologies for both decision making and implementation. The overall objective of the project is to use the research information in such a way that it matches well with the on-farm situations. Gurana village has been selected which is free from urbanization with minimum absentee landlords. The information about the agro-eco-systems and socio-economic conditions of this village has been obtained through suitable Participatory Rural Appraisal Techniques (PRA). A total of 1000 farm families have been benefitted through this project of two years duration. The soil conditions, cropping patterns, preferences to crops and requirements have been assessed. The common problems of the Gurana village have been prioritized with farmer’s participation considering their needs and resources. Hence, different technology modules encompassing areas of integrated farming has been formulated in consultation with the farmers. A participatory planning exercise involving the farmers provides basis for these modules. The modules include introduction of crops, pest and weed management practices, livestock management and activities for income augmentation to meet the needs of the farmers considering their problems, resources and willingness to act upon
