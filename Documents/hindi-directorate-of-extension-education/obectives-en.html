The Farm Information and Communication Service Wing (FICSW), unit of Directorate of Extension Education has been established with the following objectives: 

  • To provide audio-visual and mass media support to the various wings, sections and departments of the University. 
  • To organise annual krishi melas (Rabi & Kharif) at University level. 
  • To organizing agro-industrial exhibitions on various occasions and participation in national and state level exhibitions.
  • Smooth operation and management of Community Radio Station (CRS), Mobile Diagnostic cum Exhibition Van and Dr. Mangal Sein Agricultural Sciences Museum.
