FICS wing is situated at the first floor of the Directorae of Extension Education. FICS conducts Krishi Melas (Rabi & Kharif) in both the seasons with the objectives to disseminate the activities of the University and the Directorate to the rural masses and to bring back the farmers’ problems for the attention of the scientists. Dr. Mangal Sein Agriculture Sciences Museum are also maintained and supervised by this wing under the Umbrella of Directorate of Extension Education.
