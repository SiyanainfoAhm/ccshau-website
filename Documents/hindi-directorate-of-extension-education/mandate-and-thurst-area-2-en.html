Mandate/Thrust Area

  • To provide effective extension education training to middle-level extension functionaries.
  • To acquaint trainees with the sources of useful information and material related to their professional needs and help them to develop soft skills.
  • To provide a learning experience that leads to an increasing understanding of the extension education process and its application in rural development programs.
  • To develop a thorough understanding of the basic principles of learning, teaching, and extension among the trainees.
  • To acquaint trainees with the latest insights into the agricultural extension management system.
  • To promote modern tools and techniques of agricultural extension.
