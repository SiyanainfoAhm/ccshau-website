Scientific Advisory Committee Meeting

The Scientific Advisory Committee Meeting is a regular feature and being conducted at concerned KVKs once a year during the month of September/October every year at all the ICAR funded KVKs under the Chairmanship of Vice-Chancellor. In the absence of Vice-Chancellor, Director of Extension Education is the Chairman of SAC meetings. In these meetings, Senior Coordinator/Coordinator presents the salient findings and achievements of KVK particularly of various OFTs and FLDs which they conducted during both the seasons. They also present the action plan for the coming year. Progressive farmers also attend such meetings to express their views and expectations from the KVK. This is a useful platform to have interactive meeting of scientists, farmers and field staff of Deptt. of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare, Govt. of Haryana and scientists from the line departments so as to take the benefit of collective wisdom for the benefits of farming community of Haryana.
