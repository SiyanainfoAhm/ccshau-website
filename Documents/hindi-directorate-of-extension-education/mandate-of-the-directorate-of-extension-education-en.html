MANDATE OF THE DIRECTORATE 

  • Planning, organizing, conducting and coordinating the extension education activities of the university in the Haryana State.
  • To transfer technologies (developed by the university scientists) to the farmers, field staff of State Government and other agencies engaged in agriculture (including animal husbandry, horticulture and other allied areas.

To maintain collaboration/ Incharge with the State Department of Agriculture & Farmers’ Welfare, Horticulture Department, social Welfare Department (Woman Organization and Child Development) etc.
