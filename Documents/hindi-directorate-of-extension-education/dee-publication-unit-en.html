The Publication Unit is situated in the Directorate of Extension Education with the objectives to print and publish Haryana Kheti (monthly hindi magazine), preparation and publication of Annual Reports of University and any other priting works needed by the university authority and various departments.

This unit of Directorate of Extension Education has been established with the following objectives: 

  • Printing and publication of Haryana Kheti (Hindi Monthly Magazine)
  • Preparation and publication of Annual Reports of the University
  • Printing of University Diaries, Calendars etc.
  • Printing and Binding work related to documents such as certificates, answer sheets, embossing of University stamps on certificates, 
  • Printing of Pamphlets for University Krishi Mela (Rabi & Kharif), Guide books for farmers, 
  • Publication of latest version of University Telephone Directory of working as well as retired employees
