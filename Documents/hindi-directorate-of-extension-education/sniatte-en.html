Saina Nehwal Institute of Agricultural Technology, Training and Education (SNIATTE)

Saina Nehwal Institute of Agricultural Technology, Training & Education (IATTE), CCSHAU, Hisar is situated at Gate No. 3 of the University imparts trainings to farmer/farmwomen and rural youths to update their knowledge & skill and to start their enterpreneurs. SNAITTE is also an affiliated centre of Agricultural Skill Council of India (ASCI) for 19 Skill Oriented Courseson various disciplines/areas.
