Infrastructure

                HOSTEL

            The Institute has a well-furnished 24 rooms hostel for providing lodging facilities to the    participants. The hostel is used for participants of various On-campus courses organized        at the Institute. It also serves as a guest house for various dignitaries visiting the institute      for official purposes. All the rooms in the hostel have attached bathroom, geyser, A.C and     LED along with dining facilities. Wi-Fi facility is also available in the hostel premises.

                SEMINAR HALL

            The Institute has a seminar hall having capacity of 45-50 participants. It is being used for  imparting training to the participants of various courses.

                LIBRARY

            The Institute has a good collection of books on Agricultural Extension and allied   subjects. In addition to books, the library is also enriched with quarterly/ monthly    journals and magazines.

                COMPUTER LAB

            The institute has a computer lab with computers of the latest technology, internet   connectivity, and a projector for the training purpose
