Extension Activities 

The Institute organizes training and workshops for middle-level extension personnel including Subject Matter Specialists, Sub Divisional Agricultural Officers, Agriculture Development Officers, Horticulture development officers and other in-service professionals working in the Department of Agriculture, Horticulture, Animal Husbandry, Social forestry, Fisheries, Sericulture etc. of the eight northern states viz. Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttrakhand, Delhi, Bihar, Jharkhand and three union territories i.e. Jammu & Kashmir, Laddakh and Chandigarh. Along with these training programmes EEI, Nilokheri also conducts workshops for the Extension Professionals, Scientists and Master Trainers working in Agricultural Universities and Krishi Vigyan Kendras of above mentioned states.
