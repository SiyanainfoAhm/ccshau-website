T&V

Monthly training under T&V System is the most regular feature since 1978-79 conducted by this institute for the DDAs, SDAOs & SMSs from the Department of Agriculture, DHOs and HDOs from the Department of Horticulture and Extension Specialists of KVKs of Hisar zone i.e. Distt. Hisar, Sirsa, Fatehabad, Jind & Siwani Sub Division of Bhiwani. In this workshop, the feedback from the field functionaries are received and discussed with the experts of the university.                                    

ZREAC (Zonal Research and Extension Action Committee)

In order to strengthen the extension research approach, two Workshops of Zonal Research and Extension Action Committee of Hisar Zone, one each for Rabi and Kharif season are organized in the month of August and February, respectively i.e. about a month ahead of the main Agricultural Officers’ Workshop.  

Exposure visits of Farmers/ Scientists Personnel

A large number of visitors/farmers from different states of the country viz. Rajasthan, Punjab, Jammu & Kashmir, and Uttar Pradesh etc. and abroad visit the university. SNIATTE also holds the responsibility of visits to different farms and scientific spots of the university in addition to its own mandates.
