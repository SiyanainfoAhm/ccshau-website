The Farm Information and Communication Service (FICS), unit of Directorate of Extension Education started in 1976, is one of the important channels through which the activities of the University are disseminated for farming community and also to bring back the farmers’ feedback for the attention of the university scientists. The unit discharges multifarious responsibilities ranging from Annual Kirshi Melas (Rabi & Kharif),  arranging and organizing agro-industrial exhibitions at state and national level, spreading latest agricultural information through Community Radio Station, Mobile Diagnostic cum Exhibition Van and Dr. Mangal Sein Agricultural Sciences Museum.
