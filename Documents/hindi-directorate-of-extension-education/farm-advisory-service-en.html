Farm Advisory Services 

Farm Advisory Services (FAS) is a major wing of the Directorate for transfer of technology in the field of agriculture and allied discipline, it covers the entire State of Haryana  through its Farm Advisory Service Centres, popularly known as “Krishi Vigyan Kendras” (KVKs) located in each district, as well as toll free help line services ATIC (18001803001)  for disseminating relevant techniques, technologies and methods to different clientele entailing rural developments including farmers, farm women, rural youth and development agencies. The toll free help line services has also been provided of KVK, Uchani (Karnal) (18001803111)   and KVK, Bawal (Rewari) (18001804002) .

  • To organises Kisan Diwas every year on 23rd December, 2020 on the occasion of birth anniversary of late Ch. Charan Singh, Ex-Prime Minister of India.
  • To organises Agricultural Officers’ Workshop, a bi-annual feature (one for Kharif and one for Rabi crops), provides a forum for discussing the problems faced by the field staff with the university scientists about different crops and then to finalize the package of practices based on the experiences of field staff and research findings.
  • Planning and execution of extension activities of KVKs
  • Monitoring of different extension activities of KVKs under guidance of Director of Extension Education, CCSHAU, Hisar
