Objectives and Works       

  • Printing and publication of Haryana Kheti (Hindi Monthly Magazine)
  • Preparation and publication of Annual Reports of the University
  • Printing of University Diaries, Calendars etc. every year
  • Printing and Binding work related to documents such as certificates, answer sheets, embossing of University stamps on certificates, 
  • Printing of Pamphlets for University Krishi Mela (Rabi & Kharif), Guide books for farmers, Receipt register, Dispatch register, Consumable register, Non-consumable register, Contingent register, Attendance register, Student Attendance Register, Security Vehicle Register, One, two and four quire register, Stock ledger register, log book, ECR, Cash book, TA Bill Book, Station Dak Book, Complained Slip, Night Gate Pass for students, Demand Voucher, Medical Slip, MB Book, Shorthand Book, Cash Receipt Book, Data Book, Pensioner Book, Service Books, Campus school fees receipt book, Contingent Pad, Writing pad, Short Writing pad, Registration Card for student admission, Homework diary for Campus School Students, Deduction/Redrawal Bill pad, Transfer pad, Instructor’s report pad  of the University.
  • Binding work of different documents such as Thesis, Agenda, GPF and CPF ledger, Classified abstract register, Bank Scroll register of  both the University
  • Publication of latest version of University Telephone Directory of working as well as retired employees
  • All the important events of the University are covered through photography and videography by the Publication Unit.
