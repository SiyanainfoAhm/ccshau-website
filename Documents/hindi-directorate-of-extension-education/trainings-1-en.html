SNIATTE imparts the following trainings to the farmers and unemployed youth of the Haryana and adjoining states of the country. 

General Vocational Trainings

  • Bee Keeping
  • Mushroom Production
  • Fruit & Vegetable Preservation
  • Nursery Management
  • Gardener 
  • Vermicompost Preparation

Apart from the above, this institute also conducts the following vocational trainings for upliftment of SC/ST communities of Haryana.

  • Fruit & Vegetable Preservation
  • Mushroom Production
  • Safe use of Pesticides
  • Cutting & Tailoring
  • Vale addition in cereals

Skill India is an initiative of the Government of India which has been launched to empower the youth of the country with skill sets which make them more employable and more productive in their work environment. To improve skill of the unemployed youth for self employment generation, this institute conducts training on the following job roles approved by Agriculture Skill Council of India (ASCI) under Pradhan  Mantri  Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY).
