Extension Activities

  • Farmers are advised on different agricultural aspects viz. Agronomy, Soil Science,  Entomology,  Plant Pathology,  Horticulture,  Vegetables Crops,  Medicinal,  Agroforestry etc. through TollFree Helpline Number (18001803001) and virtual link (ftf-bkqv-mae) on Toll free helpline day (Monday, Wednesday & Friday from 10:00 am to 1:00 pm). 
  • Advisory services are also providedto farmers visitingat ATIC on different agricultural aspects.
  • Crop seeds, vegetable crop seeds, university literature, value added products etc. are sold to the beneficiaries.
  • Lectures were delivered by faculty members of ATIC on different agricultural aspects in liasoning with Department of Agriculture, Department of Entomology, Plant Pathology, SNIATTE, FTC Sachakhera, KVKs etc.
